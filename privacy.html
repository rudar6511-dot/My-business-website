Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

My Business respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, contact us, place an order, or create an account, and how that information is used.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may receive information such as your name, email address, WhatsApp or phone number, business name, messages, logo requirements, reference images, and account information.

2. How We Use Information

  • To respond to enquiries and support requests.
  • To process and communicate about logo or other service orders.
  • To provide account and login functionality.
  • To improve our website, services, security, and user experience.
  • To communicate with you about an enquiry or order when necessary.

3. Login and Authentication

If you use Google or another supported authentication method, authentication information is handled through our authentication provider. We use the information necessary to identify your account and provide login-related features. We do not ask you to share your Google password with us.

4. Forms and Service Requests

Information submitted through contact or order forms may be transmitted to the email/service provider configured for those forms so that we can receive and respond to your request.

5. Uploaded Files

If you voluntarily upload reference images or other files with an order, they are used for the purpose for which you submit them. Please do not upload confidential or sensitive information that is not needed for your request.

6. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website or third-party services may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to maintain login sessions and provide essential functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as authentication, email/form processing, hosting, fonts, or other technical services. Those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet transmission or online storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose it was submitted, to provide services, maintain records, resolve disputes, or meet applicable obligations.

10. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask about personal information associated with your enquiry or account, or to request correction or deletion where applicable. Some information may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact form on our website or the contact details provided on the Contact page.
